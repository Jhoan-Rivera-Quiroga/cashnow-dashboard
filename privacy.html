Privacy policy

Cash Now Dashboard · Last updated October 31, 2026

Cash Now Dashboard is Cash Now Test Strips' report of its pick-ups. It runs entirely in your browser. There's no server behind it, and nobody else receives the data it shows you.

What it can access

When you sign in with Google, the dashboard asks for one permission: access to only the Google Drive files you use with it. That means the data sheet you select in Google's file chooser. The file chooser runs on Google's site, and the dashboard learns only which file you picked. It can't see your other files, your email, your contacts or your Google profile.

How it uses the data

It reads the sheet's title, the names of its tabs and the records on its Collection tab, and shows them as charts and tables. It never changes the sheet. The data isn't sent anywhere, sold, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

What is stored

  • Nothing from the sheet is saved. The records stay in the page's memory until you close the tab or choose Sign out.
  • Google gives the page a sign-in token that lasts about an hour. It's kept only in the page's memory.
  • Your browser remembers the sheet's ID and title, so your next visit skips the file chooser. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.
  • The dashboard sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking.
  • Download CSV saves the rows you're viewing as a file on your device.

Other services

Google handles sign-in, the file chooser and reading the sheet, under Google's privacy policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and two open-source libraries load from cdn.sheetjs.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net. As with any website, those services see your IP address and browser details when your browser loads the page. They never receive data from the sheet.

If you open a workbook file instead of signing in, the file is read inside your browser and never uploaded.

Removing access

You can remove the dashboard's access at any time in your Google Account, under Third-party apps & services. The dashboard keeps no copy of your data, so there's nothing to delete on its side.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Cash Now Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions and changes

If you have a question about this policy, ask the person at Cash Now Test Strips who shared the data sheet with you. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
